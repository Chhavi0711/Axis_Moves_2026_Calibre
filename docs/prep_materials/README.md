# Prep Materials

These are the team's own initial idea and preparation documents for Axis Calibre - not
an official competition round deliverable. They're included here for anyone who wants
the full depth behind the two-slide submission, and because the case-study docs in
[`/case_study`](../../case_study/) are built directly from this material.

## Files

- **`Axis_Calibre_Master_Document.docx`** - the team's own full explanation of the idea:
  problem framing, how the system works, the operating model, customer and employee
  journeys, governance and AI architecture, the rollout plan, the five proprietary
  KPIs, all five risks and mitigations, the live narrative script used when presenting,
  slide-by-slide speaker notes, and a 25-question judge-prep list.
- **`Axis_Calibre_Deep_QA.docx`** - a deep dive on the three questions the team expected
  a sharp jury to press hardest on: exactly how the track record (Calibration Score) is
  calculated, how gaming is prevented, and why the mechanism sits inside Axis's existing
  governance rather than around it.

## An important note on the scoring in these documents

The Master Document includes a jury-score table (by criterion, with a weighted total).
**This is the team's own internal, self-assessed prediction, produced for pitch
rehearsal purposes.** No actual jury has reviewed or scored this submission yet, and
this number should not be read as, or presented as, a real result. We've kept it in the
source document as-is for an honest record of our own prep process, but it is
intentionally not repeated as a headline claim anywhere else in this repository.
